# Licensing and source provenance

## Repository content

No repository-wide license has been selected. Before public release, the repository owner should choose the intended permissions for prompts, documentation, synthesized data, map artwork, and scripts. A license may cover these differently. Public visibility alone does not grant a broad reuse license. This package deliberately avoids assigning an unapproved license or named copyright holder.

## Original research sources

Bibliographic references, URLs, and paraphrased observation records are included for traceability. Full article text, article abstracts, publisher figure images, and supplemental files are not redistributed. Scientific claims should be attributed to their original papers. Readers should obtain source material through the publishers or repositories and respect its applicable terms.

The preview images were generated from the synthesized pathway structure; they are not copies of article figures.

## GPML schema provenance

The example was format-validated using a GPML2013a XSD obtained from the PathVisio upstream project:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PathVisio/GPML/master/GPML2013a/GPML2013a.xsd

Upstream project: https://github.com/PathVisio/GPML

The third-party schema is not redistributed in this repository. To repeat optional schema validation, obtain a suitable GPML2013a XSD separately and pass its path to `scripts/validate_example.py --schema /path/to/GPML2013a.xsd`. The standard-library checks do not require it. The repository does not claim ownership of or relicense the schema.

## Repository citation

`CITATION.cff` intentionally omits author, repository URL, and DOI fields until the owner supplies verified values. Do not interpret the example date or package version as peer review, endorsement, or publication of the underlying scientific findings.
